> **A fully open-source, plug-and-play congressional campaign model designed to dismantle government bureaucracy and slash public sector wait times.**

Welcome to **The People's Congress**, a radically transparent, community-driven campaign blueprint. This repository is not funded by PACs, special interests, or corporate donors. It is a functional, modular template built to be **forked, localized, and deployed** by any grassroots candidate running for office who wants to make government work at the speed of modern life.

Our platform bypasses generic political promises to focus on structural engineering fixes for the two systems that frustrate everyday citizens the most: **Healthcare Delivery** and the **Department of Motor Vehicles (DMV)**.

---

## ⚡ Core Philosophy: The Zero-Wait State

Modern technology allows us to move money, route global logistics, and communicate instantly. Yet, citizens face systemic, life-draining delays when interacting with vital public infrastructure. This repository operates on a singular engineering principle: **Transitioning government from a high-friction bottleneck into a Zero-Wait State.**

We apply this framework to two primary targets:

### 🏥 1. Zero-Wait Healthcare (Primary Care AHEAD Integration)
* **The Problem:** Patients wait months for basic specialist referrals while independent primary care clinics drown in private insurance billing paperwork, causing rural hospital bankruptcies.
* **The Open-Source Fix:** Shifting local clinics away from "fee-for-service" and onto fixed, capitated monthly payments (inspired by the federal AHEAD model). Doctors focus on long-term prevention instead of chasing insurance claims. Global budgeting shields rural facilities from closing down due to low patient volumes.

### 🚗 2. Zero-Wait DMV (Multi-State Infrastructure Reform)
* **The Problem:** The ultimate time tax. Citizens waste half-days sitting in plastic chairs for services that should take seconds. 
* **The Open-Source Fix:** A battle-tested synthesis drawn directly from operational data across major state systems (CA, VA, and NC). We open-source a policy architecture focused on deep API integration, automated low-level data entry, expanded third-party partner networks (like VA's DMV Select), and real-time open-source wait data.

## 🛠️ How to Use This Repository

1. **Fork It:** Click the `Fork` button in the top right to create your own copy of this campaign infrastructure.
2. **Localize It:** Edit the Markdown files in `/POLICY_ENGINE` to input your specific district's rural hospital data, local DMV wait times, and unique regional challenges.
3. **Deploy It:** Use the assets and scripts in `/CAMPAIGN_OPS` to launch a modern, tech-forward campaign without hiring expensive political consultants.
